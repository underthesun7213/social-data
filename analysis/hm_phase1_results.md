## 2. Evidence sentences for the proposal

**Issue 1. Stagnant Sales and Weak Customer Retention**

- In H&M's transaction data (Sept 2018 to Sept 2020), 32.9% of 1,362,281 customers made only one purchase (same-day purchases counted as one).
- Among customers whose first purchase was before Sept 22, 2019, and who could therefore be observed for at least one year, 23.5% never returned for a second purchase; for customers who did return, the median time between purchases was 22 days.

**Issue 2. Shrinking Store Network and Shift to Online**

- In H&M's transaction data, the share of transactions made through the online channel was 85.4% in March to May 2020, compared with 70.1% in the same months of 2019.
- In April 2020, 100.0% of transactions were online. After stores reopened, the online share was 64.7% in June to August 2020, compared with 68.8% in the same months of 2019, so the shift to online in this data did not persist beyond the closure period.

**Issue 3. Customer Age Concentration**

- In H&M's customer data, the median registered age is 32, and customers aged 16 to 34 account for 55.3% of purchasing customers and 56.0% of purchase value.
- The 25-34 age group makes up 29.0% of purchasing customers but 36.8% of purchase value, with the highest purchase frequency at 29.6 transactions per customer over the two years. Customers aged 16 to 24 buy less often, at 18.4 transactions per customer.

## 3. Limitations

- **Channel:** `sales_channel_id` has no official definition. Following the common community interpretation, channel 2 is assumed to be online and channel 1 to be stores. This is an assumption.
- **Price:** `price` is a transformed value, not a real currency, so amounts are reported only as shares (%).
- **Observation window:** the data covers 2018-09-20 to 2020-09-22 only. Customers who first bought late in the window have little time to repurchase, which inflates the all-customer one-time share; the first-purchase-before-2019-09-22 cohort is reported to reduce this bias. The first and last months are partial.
- **Age:** age comes from customer registration data, not verified at purchase, and is missing for about 1.2% of customers. One account may be used by more than one person.
- **Transactions:** a transaction is one item row in transactions_train.csv; repurchase metrics use customer-days instead.
- The dataset is a sample released for a Kaggle competition and may not represent all H&M markets or sales.
